# Sprint Working Model

## Sprint Goal
One coherent objective for the Sprint.

## Sprint Backlog
- Sprint Goal
- selected PBIs
- executable plan/tasks
- current dependencies/blockers

## Daily Scrum
Inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the plan.

## Sprint Review
Inspect the Increment with stakeholders and adapt future backlog based on evidence.

## Retrospective
Inspect the team's way of working and choose concrete improvements.
